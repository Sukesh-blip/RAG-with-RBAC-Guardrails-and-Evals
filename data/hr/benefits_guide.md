# Employee Benefits Guide — NovaGrid Technologies

## Health Insurance
NovaGrid covers 100% of premiums for employee-only health insurance plans, and 70%
of premiums for dependents, through Meridian Health Group.

## Retirement Contributions
NovaGrid matches employee retirement contributions up to 4% of base salary, vested
immediately with no waiting period.

## Wellness Stipend
Employees receive a $75/month wellness stipend, usable for gym memberships, fitness
apps, or mental health services, reimbursed through the WorkSuite expense portal.

## Equity Refresh Grants
Employees rated "Exceeds Expectations" or higher in their annual review are eligible
for an equity refresh grant, sized based on role level and reviewed by HR and Finance
jointly during the annual compensation planning cycle each November.

## Enrollment Windows
Open enrollment for benefits changes occurs each October, with changes taking effect
January 1. Qualifying life events allow enrollment changes outside this window.
