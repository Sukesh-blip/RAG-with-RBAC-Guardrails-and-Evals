# Performance Review Policy — NovaGrid Technologies

## Review Cycle
NovaGrid conducts performance reviews twice a year: a mid-year check-in (June) and
an annual review (December) tied to compensation decisions.

## Rating Scale
Employees are rated on a 5-point scale:
1. Below Expectations
2. Partially Meets Expectations
3. Meets Expectations
4. Exceeds Expectations
5. Outstanding

## Compensation Linkage
Annual review ratings feed directly into merit increase and bonus calculations.
A rating of "Exceeds Expectations" or higher qualifies an employee for consideration
in the annual promotion cycle, subject to manager and HR business partner sign-off.

## Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)
Employees rated "Below Expectations" for two consecutive review cycles are placed on
a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan, managed jointly by the employee's manager and
an HR business partner, with progress checkpoints every 2 weeks.

## Confidentiality
Individual performance ratings and PIP details are confidential HR records accessible
only to the employee, their manager, and HR — not to Finance or other departments.
