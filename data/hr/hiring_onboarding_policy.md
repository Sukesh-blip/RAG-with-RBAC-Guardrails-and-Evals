# Hiring and Onboarding Policy — NovaGrid Technologies

## Requisition Approval
All new headcount requisitions require approval from the hiring manager, department
VP, and Finance (for budget confirmation) before a role can be posted externally.

## Interview Process
Standard interview loops consist of a recruiter screen, hiring manager interview,
2-3 panel interviews (role-dependent), and a final interview with a department lead
or executive for senior roles (Director level and above).

## Offer Approval
Offers above the standard band for a role require VP and HR leadership sign-off.
Equity grant sizing for new hires is determined jointly by HR and Finance based on
level, role scarcity, and current company equity pool availability.

## Onboarding
New hires complete a 2-week structured onboarding program covering company systems
access, benefits enrollment, security training, and role-specific ramp-up sessions
coordinated by their manager and an assigned onboarding buddy.

## Background Checks
All offers are contingent on successful completion of a background check, processed
through a third-party vendor and reviewed by HR prior to the employee's start date.
