# Product Roadmap — H2 2026

## Q3 2026 Priorities
- Launch NovaGrid Insights v2 with predictive anomaly detection
- Ship native integrations with Snowflake and Databricks
- Beta release of the NovaGrid Marketplace partner API

## Q4 2026 Priorities
- General availability of the Marketplace partner API
- Expand APAC data residency options (Singapore region)
- Launch self-serve onboarding flow for mid-market customers

## Long-Term Themes
The product organization is investing heavily in AI-assisted workflows across the
platform, with a goal of reducing customer time-to-value from an average of 6 weeks
to under 2 weeks by end of FY2027.
