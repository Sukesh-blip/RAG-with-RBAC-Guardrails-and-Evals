# Remote Work Policy — NovaGrid Technologies

## Eligibility
Remote work eligibility depends on role type. Engineering, Design, and most
individual-contributor roles are eligible for a hybrid (3 days office) or full-remote
arrangement by manager approval. Customer-facing and office-dependent roles follow
department-specific guidelines.

## Equipment
NovaGrid provides a one-time $500 home office stipend for remote and hybrid employees,
covering items such as monitors, chairs, and desks, reimbursed through the WorkSuite
expense portal.

## Core Hours
All employees, regardless of location, are expected to be available during core
collaboration hours of 10 AM–3 PM in their local time zone's primary office overlap
window, to support cross-team meetings.

## Time Zone Considerations
Teams spanning Bangalore and Austin offices are expected to coordinate meeting times
that provide reasonable overlap; recording and sharing meeting notes is standard
practice for teams with limited overlap windows.
