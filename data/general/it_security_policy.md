# IT Security Policy — NovaGrid Technologies

## Access Management
All employees authenticate via single sign-on (SSO) with mandatory multi-factor
authentication (MFA). Access to internal systems follows least-privilege principles,
reviewed quarterly by IT Security.

## Device Policy
Company-issued laptops must have disk encryption, endpoint detection software, and
automatic OS updates enabled at all times. Personal devices are not permitted to
access internal systems containing customer data.

## Data Classification
Internal data is classified into three tiers: Public, Internal, and Restricted.
Restricted data (financial records, employee PII, customer data) requires role-based
access controls and is never to be shared over unencrypted channels.

## Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident (phishing attempt, lost device, unauthorized access)
must be reported to security@novagrid-example.com within 1 hour of discovery.
