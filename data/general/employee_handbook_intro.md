# Employee Handbook — Introduction

## Welcome to NovaGrid Technologies
NovaGrid Technologies builds AI-powered infrastructure tooling for enterprise
platform teams. Founded in 2019, we now serve over 400 enterprise customers across
North America, Europe, and APAC.

## Company Values
1. Ship with ownership — we favor small teams shipping real outcomes over process.
2. Default to transparency — internal metrics, roadmaps, and decisions are shared
   openly across the company unless there's a specific reason not to.
3. Customer obsession over internal politics.

## Working Hours & Location
NovaGrid operates a hybrid model: employees are expected in the Bangalore or Austin
office 3 days per week, with flexibility for fully remote roles by exception.

## Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect.
Violations of the code of conduct should be reported to HR or through the anonymous
ethics hotline listed on the internal wiki.
