# New Hire Offer Economics — Engineering Org (Q3 2026)

## Hiring Volume
Engineering extended 14 offers in Q3 2026, with an 86% acceptance rate (12 accepted).

## HR-Managed Details
Offer bands were set per NovaGrid's leveling framework, with 3 offers requiring VP
exception approval for above-band equity grants due to competitive counter-offers.

## Finance-Managed Details
Total incremental annualized payroll impact from Q3 Engineering hires: $1.68M.
This was tracked against the FY2026 Engineering headcount budget, which had $2.1M
in remaining allocated headcount spend entering Q3 — leaving $420K of budget
headroom remaining for Q4 hiring.

## Notes
Any offer requiring above-band equity must be co-approved by HR leadership and
Finance before being extended to the candidate.
