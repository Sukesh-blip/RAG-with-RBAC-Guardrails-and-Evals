# HR Systems Vendor Spend Review — 2026

## Overview
This review covers vendor contracts jointly owned by HR (system usage/ownership) and
Finance (payment processing and budget tracking).

## WorkSuite HRIS
- Annual license cost: $54,300 (billed quarterly, paid by Finance)
- Owned and configured by HR Operations
- Covers payroll processing, leave management, and benefits enrollment

## Meridian Health Group (Benefits Insurance)
- Annual premium spend: $1.94M, split 100% employee-only / 70% dependent coverage
  per the Employee Benefits Guide
- Contract renewal negotiated jointly by HR and Finance each Q3

## Background Check Vendor (Verifyd Inc.)
- Per-check cost: $45, approximately 180 checks/year
- Annual spend: ~$8,100, billed to the HR cost center but paid through Finance
  accounts payable

## Renewal Timeline
All three contracts are up for renewal review in Q4 2026, requiring joint sign-off
from HR leadership and the VP of Finance before renewal.
