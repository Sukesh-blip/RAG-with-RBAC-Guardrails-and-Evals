# Annual Compensation & Budget Planning Report — November 2026 Cycle

## Purpose
This report is jointly prepared by HR and Finance each November to align the
upcoming year's compensation budget with departmental financial forecasts.

## HR Inputs
- Total eligible employees for merit increases: 198
- Average targeted merit increase: 4.2%
- Employees flagged for promotion consideration: 27
- Estimated equity refresh pool needed: 340,000 shares

## Finance Inputs
- Total FY2027 compensation budget allocated: $31.2M (from the FY2027 Budget Forecast)
- Compensation budget represents 43% of total operating expense
- Contingency reserve held for off-cycle adjustments: $600K

## Joint Sign-Off
Final compensation budget numbers require sign-off from both the VP of HR and the
CFO before merit increase letters are distributed to managers in early December.
