# FY2027 Budget Forecast — NovaGrid Technologies

## Overview
This document outlines the proposed departmental budget allocations for fiscal year 2027,
pending board approval at the October 2026 meeting.

## Proposed Departmental Budgets
- Engineering: $22.4M (+14% YoY)
- Sales & Marketing: $16.8M (+9% YoY)
- Customer Success: $6.2M (+18% YoY)
- G&A (includes HR, Finance, Legal): $9.1M (+6% YoY)
- Infrastructure & Cloud Costs: $8.7M (+22% YoY, driven by AI workload scaling)

## Revenue Targets
FY2027 revenue target is set at $92M, representing 24% growth over the FY2026 projected
close of $74.2M.

## Key Assumptions
- Net revenue retention held at 118%
- Headcount growth capped at 15% company-wide
- Cloud infrastructure costs assumed to grow faster than headcount due to increased
  AI/ML workload adoption across product lines

## Risk Factors
Finance flags foreign exchange exposure on APAC contracts (approximately 12% of revenue)
as the primary risk to the forecast, given currency volatility observed in Q2-Q3 2026.
