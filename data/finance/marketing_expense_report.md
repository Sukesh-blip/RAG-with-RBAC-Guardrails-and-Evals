# Marketing Expense Report — August 2026

## Total Spend
Total marketing spend for August 2026 was $612,400, against a monthly budget of $650,000
(94.2% budget utilization).

## Breakdown by Channel
- Paid search (Google/Bing): $198,200
- Paid social (LinkedIn/Meta): $154,600
- Content & SEO tooling: $61,000
- Events & sponsorships: $112,300
- Marketing automation platform (annual license, amortized): $38,500
- Agency retainer (Bright Path Creative): $47,800

## Campaign ROI Notes
The "Foundry Launch" campaign (paid search + LinkedIn) generated 1,240 marketing qualified
leads at a blended CAC of $94, a 21% improvement over the Q2 average CAC of $119.

## Budget Variance
Events & sponsorships ran $18,300 over the allocated $94,000 budget due to an unplanned
booth upgrade at the DevSummit 2026 conference. Finance has approved the overage as a
one-time exception.
