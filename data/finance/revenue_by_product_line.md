# Revenue by Product Line — H1 2026

## Product Line Performance
- NovaGrid Core (platform subscriptions): $19.8M, 61% of total revenue
- NovaGrid Insights (analytics add-on): $6.4M, 20% of total revenue
- NovaGrid Marketplace (commission-based): $4.1M, 13% of total revenue
- Professional Services: $1.9M, 6% of total revenue

## Growth Trends
NovaGrid Insights showed the strongest YoY growth at 47%, driven by new enterprise
customer adoption following the Q1 2026 feature expansion into predictive analytics.

## Customer Concentration
The top 10 customers account for 31% of total revenue. No single customer exceeds
6% of total revenue, keeping concentration risk within Finance's internal threshold
of 8% per account.
