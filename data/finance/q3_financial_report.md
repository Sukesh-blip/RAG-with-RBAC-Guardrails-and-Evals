# Q3 2026 Financial Report — NovaGrid Technologies

## Summary
NovaGrid Technologies closed Q3 2026 with total revenue of $18.4M, up 12% quarter-over-quarter.
Operating expenses came in at $13.1M, resulting in an operating margin of 28.8%.

## Revenue Breakdown
- Product subscriptions: $11.2M
- Professional services: $4.6M
- Marketplace commissions: $2.6M

## Operating Expenses
- R&D: $5.8M
- Sales & Marketing: $4.2M
- General & Administrative: $2.1M
- Customer Support: $1.0M

## Cash Position
Cash and cash equivalents stood at $42.3M at quarter end, with a burn rate of approximately
$1.1M/month against current runway projections of 38 months at present spend levels.

## Notable Items
The Finance team flagged a one-time $380K vendor settlement related to a legacy data
center contract, recorded as a non-recurring expense in G&A.
